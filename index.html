What it is

Each card has a small NFC chip built in. Hold it near the front or back of a phone and it opens whatever you've linked it to, a social profile, a contact card, or a page with all your links in one place. No signal, no data, no battery. It works for years.

You can change what the card opens whenever you want. Switched platforms, changed your number, rebranded your page? We update the link, the physical card stays the same.
